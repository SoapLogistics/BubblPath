# GABRIEL OS KERNEL: NEXT 10 EVOLUTION STEPS (Phases 21-30)

Building on the 20-phase evolution, these next 10 steps drive the system toward deeper self-regulation and advanced dynamic execution.

## Execution & Resource Management (Phases 21-24)
21. **Adaptive Worker Timeouts:** `GabrielKernel` dynamically adjusts execution timeouts based on the predicted complexity score of the incoming task.
22. **Context Importance Weighting:** `DynamicContextEngine` now assigns importance scores to messages, ensuring critical system context is never pruned before low-value chat chatter.
23. **Graph Relationship Strength:** `UniversalKnowledgeGraph` edges are upgraded from simple labels to weighted relationships, enabling shortest-path relevance scoring.
24. **Energy-Aware Routing:** `GabrielKernel` monitors `UnifiedDashboard`'s kW/h metrics; if system energy usage breaches a threshold, tasks are aggressively routed to the cheapest, fastest `LocalStubWorker`.

## Knowledge & Skill Maturation (Phases 25-27)
25. **Skill Dependency Chaining:** `SkillAssimilation` tracks which skills depend on other skills, allowing for modular execution of complex workflows.
26. **Priority Curiosity Queue:** `CuriosityEngine` upgrades from a standard list to a priority queue, sorting research tasks by their failure severity or context gaps.
27. **Dynamic API Generation:** `SolomonOSKernel` allows newly benchmarked skills to automatically register their own Flask REST endpoints on the fly.

## Telemetry & Resilience (Phases 28-30)
28. **Latency-Based Precision Downgrade:** `QuantizationCore` continuously monitors routing latency; if thresholds are breached, it aggressively forces 4-bit quantization regardless of available VRAM.
29. **Anomaly Alerting:** `UnifiedDashboard` fires warning alerts if error rates or RAM spikes suddenly deviate from historical moving averages.
30. **Federated Knowledge Sync:** `UniversalKnowledgeGraph` introduces a stub for federated peer-to-peer synchronization, allowing multiple Gabriel nodes to merge their graph structures.
